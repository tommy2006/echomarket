# 🛒 Echo Market · Buyers' Guide
Order aircraft from the alliance's sellers, *below the in-game list price*.

## 1 · Sign in
Open **<https://echomarket-buyer.vercel.app>** and press **Sign in with Discord**. Only members of this server can use the market.
-# The market rules pop up on your first visit; they're always behind the 📜 button at the top.

## 2 · Create your airline
**Airlines → New airline**: name + alliance (up to 20 profiles). Use **Rename / edit** to update a profile.

## 3 · Place an order
**Shop** → pick a model, the quantity and a **price level** (50–90 % of the list price).
Open your order (🛒), tick the airline(s) that may receive the planes, then **Send order**.
-# Limits: $10B per 24 hours (counted at list price) · 5 orders per 10 minutes.

**On Discord instead?** In the ordering channel:
> `/airline create` → make a profile
> `/order` → up to 3 aircraft types, ➕ **Add aircraft** for more, then **Send order**

## 4 · Receive your planes
When a seller takes your order you get a **DM**. They list the aircraft on the in-game market for you, so ***buy exactly what they listed.***
If more sellers join (*"… will also be delivering"*), your order shows **how many each seller listed**. Buy from each one accordingly.

## 5 · Need help?
Not fully delivered after **24 hours**? Open it under **Orders → Contact the seller team**.
Planes gone before you could buy them? Press **Report missing planes** on the order and say how many and which type. *The sellers check in-game who took them.*

> ⚠️ Only buy planes **you** ordered, and never re-sell market planes outside the alliance.
