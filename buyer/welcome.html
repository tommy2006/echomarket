TWR · CLEARANCE

The market rules

The market rules are shown in English: that is the official wording.

  1. No arguments over the price or amount of aircraft(s).
  2. Do not re-sell the planes that you have bought through the market to outside. (You can sell them back in our alliance if you do not need the planes anymore, but you must sell them back at the percentage which you purchased it at)
  3. Do not buy any planes from the market if you did not order them.This is why you are here. The rest of this walk is about rule 3.
  4. Refrain from having a full-on conversation within the tickets.

TWY A · HOW DELIVERY WORKS

Your planes are listed in public

01 / 03

Taken

A seller takes your order and buys your aircraft new in-game.

02 / 03

Listed

They list them on the public in-game market under their airline (not their Discord username), at your price level. You get a DM / order update saying what was listed and by which airline.

03 / 03

Bought

You search for that airline, then buy exactly your model and quantity, straight away.

Because the market is public, anyone browsing can see these listings. That's why everyone must only buy their own.

HOLD SHORT · WHAT COUNTS AS STEALING

Four ways to take someone's planes

  1. A cheap listing that isn't yours

    A discounted A350 from an airline that isn't your seller, and not in #ffa-listings, belongs to another buyer, however tempting.

  2. More than you ordered

    Your order is 4× A320neo, and your seller lists 6. The extra 2 are someone else's.

  3. The wrong model

    Sellers often list several models for several buyers at once. Buy only the models in your order.

  4. Buying for someone else

    Grabbing listings "for a friend" or for another account of yours still takes someone's order.

FFA · OPEN STAND

Free-for-all listings

Read existing FFA sales carefully. Only that channel, #ffa-listings: all planes listed there are free for all.

If an airplane is not under FFA, it is ordered by someone other than you.

If unsure whether an airplane is FFA, feel free to ask on #market-chat.

MAYDAY · IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Mistakes, missing planes, consequences

Bought planes that weren't yours by mistake?
Tell #market-chat or an admin right away, and follow the seller team's instruction (for example: re-list the same planes at the same price for the real buyer). Owning up quickly counts in your favour.
Your planes were gone before you could buy them?
Press Report missing planes on your order and say how many and which type. The sellers check in-game who bought them.
Order not fully delivered after 24 hours?
Use Contact the seller team on the order.

Consequences: a confirmed theft gives a warning, and repeat cases lead to market bans, fully at the admins' discretion. Every incident is recorded on your disciplinary record. You must follow the seller team's follow-up instruction (it could be a re-list).
